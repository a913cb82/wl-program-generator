## Doctrine

1. One change in one commit. A mixed commit teaches nothing.
2. Measure on the fixed exam only. Explorer reports are not scores.
3. Never edit the scorer or its weights. The ruler stays frozen.
4. Gate on exc (stable), never on book jitter (±0.003 run-to-run noise).
5. Co-host-only: never add a movement to a session.
6. Recon is the orchestrator's job. Briefs carry exact anchors and carry at most two edits.
