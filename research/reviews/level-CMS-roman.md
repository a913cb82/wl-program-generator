# Level CMS — OURS vs Roman (adversarial review)

Scope: `outputs/CMS.json` (89 sess, 6712 lifts) vs Roman I/CMS norms. Severity-ranked.

## 1. Squat volume light by ~5pp (worst mix miss)
- OURS Squat (=front/back+other) 1508/6712 = **22.5%**. Roman Table 11:
front+back **23%** + other ~**6%** prep / ~4% comp = ~**29%**/~**27%** total.
- Deficit ≈ 4–6pp (~300–400 lifts). All other groups match Table 11:
snatch 20.4% vs 20/21; cleans 15.3% vs 15/15; jerks 12.3% vs 12/13;
SnPull 6.5% vs 7/5; ClPull 5.4% vs 5/6; Fundamental 17.7% vs 18/17.

## 2. Month volumes inside range but below Roman averages
- Roman: prep avg **1650** (range 1300–2000), comp avg **1200** (950–1450) (p88, p91).
- OURS mesos: 1342 / 1477 / 1678 / 1208 / 1007. Prep avg **1499** (−9%);
comp avg **1108** (−8%). Only meso-2 hits the prep mean; meso-4 near comp floor.

## 3. Top-end (90%+) share ~half of Roman comp norm
- OURS Snatch zones [167,257,154,20,8]: 90%+ = 28/606 = **4.6%** vs Roman
I/CMS Middle prep 26/330 = 7.9%, comp 23/252 = **9.1%** (Table 2, p46).
- OURS Jerk 90%+ = 22/465 = **4.7%** vs prep 10/198 = 5.1% (ok) but comp
14/156 = **9.0%** (Table 4, p56) — comp peak halved.
- Quota anchor (p130–137): last-prep 378 snatch+C&J (227/106/45); comp 328 (187/91/50). OURS has no 90%+ spike.

## 4. Final-week % correct, but exclusion rules violated
- OURS last weeks: m3w3 145/1208 = **12.0%**, m4w3 131/1007 = **13.0%** —
inside Roman 11–14% (p105); week splits match worked examples exactly
(m3 = 36/28/24/12 low-class comp p118; m4 = 28/33/26/13 Class-I comp p139).
- BUT last-week sessions still carry top-end sets, e.g. `m4 w3 d2`:
`Fundamental … [80,2]×5, [90,2]`, `Squat … [90,1]`; `d0` ClPull `[0,3,3,2,0]`.
Roman: C&J 90%+ excluded wk4 (p140–141), pull 100%+ excluded wk4 (p110,
36/33/31/0%), squat 100%+ wk4 ~6% (~4 lifts, p137).

## 5. Minor: raw 4-2 opener; push-jerk CMS+2% untestable

## What matches (do not regress)
- Classic shares: snatch 606/1370 = **44.2%** (45/43); clean **40.4%** (40); jerk **56.2%** (58/54).
- Session texture, e.g. `meso0 w0 d0` (85 lifts):
`SnPull+Snatch [60,[2,3]] [70,[2,3]] [80,[2,1]] …; Other Clean+Other Jerk
[70,[2,1]]×7 + [80,[1,1]]×5; ClPull [80,3] [90,2]×2; Fundamental [80,2]×8` —
reps/set legal per Table 1 (1–2 @70–79, 1 @80–89) and Pull rules (p63–64).
- Sizes min 26 / max 145 / avg 75.4 fit S/M/L cutoffs (p106).

Verdict: ship-blocking = #1 (squat −5pp) + #3 (comp 90%+ halved); move ~350 lifts Squat-ward, double comp 90%+, enforce wk4 exclusions.
