## 4. Peaking instruments
- **Takano:** complexes ARE the peak. MS meso-1: C+J 47/88 sets ≥90, OthC+Jerk 24/34 ≥90 — all [1,1]; m1w2d3 drops to a single [90,[1,1]] + 12×Squat@90. CMS meso-1 same (C+J 20/47 ≥90).
- **Ours:** no complex peaking. 90s are ~15–19% of complex sets, always embedded in ramps (MS C+J 40/207, OthC+OthJ 79/358); taper is cut by dropping sets, never by flat-90 complex blocks.

## 5. Verdict
- **Copy:** flat-block complex peaking — end each cycle with 1–2 weeks of 6–9×[90,[1,1]] Clean+Jerk (+ variant on light day), mirroring the Snatch ladder above it.
- **Kill:** our 12–18-set complex marathons (e.g. CMS SnPull+OthSnatch ×16–17, C+J ×18) — cap complexes at 9 sets; buy volume back with a second single-lift slot the way Takano pairs Snatch then C+J.
