# Method: Roman's Construction vs OURS (deep dive)

## 1. Direction: top-down vs bottom-up
Roman (selection §§95–113, volumes, intensity §§4–6): **month type → Table-13 variant → week loads → workout composition → set schemes.**
Month total and Table-11 shares fixed first (e.g. 75kg low-class 1250 prep / 900 comp).
Variant chosen **by history**: after large-volume month only 2, 3-1, 2-4, 4-2; after moderate/small any (p106).
Week loads cut into S (50) / M (51–100) / L (100+) combos with rest logic, then days (e.g. wk2 Mon130/Tue40/Wed95/Fri135/Sat50, ≤2 L/wk low-class).
Workout composition follows speed→strength→endurance order (strength-first only for strength emphasis), placement rules (power snatch before snatch, pulls middle/end, jerks middle=strength / end=endurance), then Table-1 reps/set and Tables-2–6 zone counts.
OURS (`build_plan`): **annual → meso spread → week variants → sessions → zones → sets.**
`annual` × `macro_days`/365 → `allocate` across MACRO splits → `spread()` re-spreads remaining meso target within TOL band → `week_sessions` first-feasible S/M/L template → `fit_session`/IPF group×session matrix → `week_bin_lifts` (yearly BINS shifted by GAP/tilt) → `zone_alloc` + set rendering.
Where it matters: Roman's week shape is **backward-looking** (prior month) and **backward-planned** (lag table from comp date fixes when last 90/95/100% may occur, §5); ours is **forward-allocated** with fixed MACRO splits and no memory. Roman derives zones from **target result** (MS90 +10kg→~340 high-intensity lifts via Fig.19, then +13.5% over Tables-2–4); we derive zones from **annual-average BINS shifted by one ARI gap**, so volume decides intensity, never the reverse.

## 2. No counterpart vs fake counterpart
No counterpart in our chain: (a) **variant selection by history** (large-prior restricts to 2/3-1/2-4/4-2); (b) **AM/PM two-a-day splits** — snatch AM / squats 2×PM prep, classic clean/jerk uniform comp, snatch-pull AM + clean-pull PM (p145–146); (c) **concur/contrast choice** — volume variant × independent intensity variant (e.g. vol 2-4 × int 4-1; vol 3-1 × int 1-3 putting min high-intensity 83 in max-volume wk 640); (d) **weight-class final-week variants** — lights max-intensity day 3 (70/50/36/rest/comp) vs mid-heavy/heavy day 1 (50/65/40/35/comp), contrast-directional preferred; (e) **lag enforcement** — last 90/95/100% day-windows per lift (snatch 5–9/6–12/7–15d, C&J 5–13/7–15/9–18d, squat/pull tables), lighter-later, C&J-longer, shock on comp weekday.
Fake counterparts (same shape, different cause): our **taper** (zero all 90%+ Snatch/CJ wk4) mimics his last-week cap but his is a **10% snatch/C&J + 6% squat allowance with excess transferred forward** and class-timed, not a blanket delete; our **BW_TILT** (±0.5 ARI) mimics his class corrections but his are **zone-count deltas** (−2–3 @70–79, −4–5 @80–89 for 67.5 vs 60kg; pulls/squats separate) with opposite 100%+ rules; our **S/M/L VARIANTS table** mimics his combos but his encode recovery (Sunday rest/comp, ≤2 L, post-comp 3d rest), ours is first-feasible-capacity.

## 3. Repair stack: ladder logic or scaffolding?
Roman would recognize the *goal* (contiguous warmup ramps; jerk ladders +≤5%/set) but not the *mechanism*. His gaps are intentional: skip min/small after power snatch, start moderate after similar-structure work, never put submax/max mid/end. Our `close_bins` / `close_session` / `close_week` / `close_ramps` + the `plan_sessions` ramp-repair block (lone-zone teleport to base, singleton-block shift) exist because IPF/`fit` + MIN_DOSE + DAY-template slot clashes produce gapped, stranded matrices — they move single reps to fake contiguity while claiming totals exact.
Worst offender: **`close_ramps`** — two-way swaps plus three-cycles churn lifts *between days* until every session is gap-free from its base, gated only by `weekcount[z] >= spanning(z)`. It silently undoes `zone_alloc`'s peak (prep-concentrate / comp-spread) and hides the real failure: the week promised more sessions spanning a zone than the zone holds. Roman would fix the week shape; we rearrange deck chairs one rep at a time.

## 4. The one principle change
**Plan backwards from the competition date with intensity quotas primary.** Fix comp day → final-week allowance (10%/6%) → place each lift's last 90/95/100% by the lag table → choose the Table-13 variant by prior-month size → set month 70%+/90%+ counts from Tables-2–6 × target-result (Fig.19) × class/age corrections → let volume follow. Today volume is allocated and zones are shifted to fit; Roman fits volume to placed heavy lifts.
