# Complexes: Roman doctrine vs OURS (READ-ONLY audit)

## 1. Roman complex rulebook (numbered, page refs)
1. Allowed pairs are closed: front-squat+push-jerk/jerk; squat-clean+front-squat+jerk 1+2+1 (p60-61); beginner lesson power-clean+push-press 1+3 (vol p112-114).
2. Full classic C&J is NOT a daily complex: complete lift every 7-10d; otherwise in PARTS (cleans or jerks) (p55-60).
3. Classic jerk usually from stands, not after clean; if jerk faulty, +<=5%/set (p57-59). Rack-jerk and full-C&J counted separately (Tables 3 vs 4; p52-56).
4. Counting is rep-by-rep per side, e.g. 140/2+2, 150/1+2+1 (p60-61). Table 1 (p46) applies PER SIDE: clean/jerk 2-3 @60-69.9%, 1-2 @70-79.9%, 1 @80-89.9%.
5. Ratios favor squats/jerks, not cleans: 2+2, 1+2+1, 1+3. Never 3 cleans + N jerks in one set.
6. Weight band: combos at 45-80% limit C&J (ex: 90/45%...150/75%, 140/70%...160/80%) (p60-61). No 90%+ complexes.
7. Placement: strength complexes mid-workout; endurance variants end-workout <=85%, 4-6 sets (p60: 77.5/65% 6x3-4; p60: 90/75% 1+2+1 mid).
8. Pulls are STANDALONE (middle/end, 15-30 @2-4/set; p64), never `Pull+X` in one set. Low-class: NO pulls at all; power snatch/clean suffice (p61).
9. Zones: snatch/pull-% of limit snatch, clean/jerk/squat-% of limit C&J (p79). Complex % = of C&J (p60-61 ex. /200, /120, /150).
10. Power variants are separate exercises before the classic (6-15 power reps, then +5-15 kg into classic; p59), not fused as `PowerClean+Jerk` sets.

## 2. Our violations (severity-ranked)
1. SEVERE — Pull+lift complexes with no precedent, at all qualified classes: `SnPull+Snatch 60/[2,3]` (I m0w0d0); `SnPull+Other Snatch 60/[1,2]x16 sets incl 90/[1,1]` (CMS m0w1d2); `ClPull+Other Clean 60/[3,1]` (CMS m0w3d4). Roman: pulls standalone (rule 8).
2. SEVERE — 3-clean sides violating Table-1-per-side: `Other Clean+Other Jerk 60/[3,2]` (III m0w1d2); `Clean+Jerk 60/[3,3] 70/[3,3]` (III m3w3d5); `Other Clean+Jerk 60/[3,2]` (II m0w0d2). Roman max 2-3 @60s only as singles, never 3+3.
3. HIGH — Full `Clean+Jerk` as routine low-class slot (III/II n=4 each, incl 90/[1,1] at III m3w3d5). Roman: full C&J every 7-10d; parts otherwise (rule 2); low-class 100% 1x/10-14d (p57).
4. HIGH — 90%+ inside complexes: `Clean+Jerk ...90/[1,1]x5` (MS m2w2d5); `Other Clean+Other Jerk ...90/[1,1]x2` (MS m2w0d1); `SnPull+Other Snatch ...90/[1,1]x5` (MS m2w2d1). Roman: combos <=80% (rule 6); 90%+ governed by lag rules p109-110.
5. MED — Inverted ratios (cleans >= jerks): `Clean+Jerk 60/[2,3]` (CMS m0w0d3, I m0w0d3, MS m0w1d5); `Other Clean+Jerk 60/[1,2]` (MS m0w0d4). Roman: 1+2+1 / 2+2 / 1+3 (rule 5).
6. MED — Rack vs full conflation: `Clean+Other Jerk 60/[3,2]` (I/CMS m1w0d3), `Other Clean+Jerk 60/[3,2]` (II/III). Roman keeps classic-jerk-from-stands separate from after-clean jerks (rule 3).
7. LOW — Complex as opener with fatigued reps: `SnPull+Snatch` slot 1 (I/CMS/MS m0w0d0/1). Roman: power-then-classic sequencing with +5-15 kg jump, not fused sets (rule 10).

## 3. Roman habits we lack entirely
- Front-squat+push-jerk/jerk 2+2 @70-80% mid-workout strength complex (p60-61). Zero emissions.
- Squat-clean+front-squat+jerk 1+2+1 (p60-61). Zero emissions.
- Power-clean+push-press 1+3 beginner complex (vol p112-114). Zero emissions.
- End-of-workout strength-endurance complex: clean/jerk <=85% 4-6 sets (p60). We never emit end-place <=85% complexes; ours sit anywhere at any zone.

## 4. Our habits with no Roman precedent
- `SnPull+Snatch`, `SnPull+Other Snatch`, `ClPull+Other Clean` as fused sets (I/CMS/MS: 21/10/5, 19/11/6, 29/19/2 slots). Roman: pulls never combined.
- `Other Clean+Other Jerk` as the DEFAULT complex (III 13, II 13, I 23, CMS 21, MS 52 slots). Roman has no power-clean+push-jerk daily complex at these volumes/ratios.
- Multi-clean endurance complexes `2+3`, `3+3` @60-70% (III m3w3d5; CMS m0w0d3). Roman: 3+3 never appears; Table 1 forbids.
- 90%+ singles inside complexes (MS/I/CMS examples above). Roman: no precedent.
