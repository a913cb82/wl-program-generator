# Level III: OURS vs TAKANO (adversarial, read-only)

Scope: ours 20-wk macrocycle (71 sess) vs Takano 8-wk pre-comp (26 sess). Reps from slots; complexes split (e.g. [3,2]→3+2). Normalised by weekly rate + shares.
1. [SEV-1] Squat overweight + pulls absent. Ours Squat 26.7% + OthSquat 14.9% = 41.6% of 4986; Tak 11.1%+13.0%=24.1% of 1420. Ours pulls 0 reps; Tak SnPull 9.2% (131) + ClPull 7.3% (103) = 16.5%, 4×3/4 @80 (88%/84% of pull reps at 80) nearly every session. Not block-explained (pulls peak pre-comp; a year-plan should have more, not zero).
2. [SEV-1] Weekly rate +40% with no taper logic. Ours 249/wk, 70.2/sess; Tak 177.5/wk, 54.6/sess. Tak taper (m1w3d3: 23 reps; m1w3d1: 28) explains ~15% of gap only; Tak base weeks already 52–110 vs ours Wednesdays 119–148. Ours ends m4w3d5 at 36, m4w3d2 at 24 — deload without supercompensation structure.
3. [SEV-2] Classics cooler at top, hotter in bulk. 90%+ share — Snatch ours 2.8% vs Tak 7.6%; Clean 3.2% vs 7.6%; Jerk 2.7% vs 7.3%; pulls 0% vs ~8%. Mean zone Snatch ours 67.3 (mode 60, 49%) vs Tak 71.2 (60/70/80 ≈ 32/30/30%). Modal classic ours always 60 (44–52%); Tak variants 60 (65–68%) but classics centred 70–80. Pre-comp should be heavier than base — inverted.
4. [SEV-2] Session shape: few-huge vs many-even. Mov/sess ours 3.41 vs Tak 4.15. Ours m4w1d2: 148 reps (Squat 17 sets/65 reps incl 60×7,60×7; OthClean+OthJerk 9×[3,1]). Ours m1w0d2: 145 (Squat 19 sets/56). Tak max m0w1d1: 110 reps over 7 movs (Squat 5×4 @60/70, Snatch 6×3, Press 4×4 @80). Ours small days m2w3d2-adjacent Fri: 39–44 reps/2–3 movs (e.g. m1w2d5 Snatch 4×3-1 + Clean 7 sets + OthSquat 7 sets). Tak small = planned taper singles (m1w2d0: Sn/C&J/OthSquat all 1s and 2s, Sn 7×1, C+J 7×[1,1] to 90).
5. [SEV-3] Reps/set habits. Ours: squat sets of 5–8 (60×7,60×8,70×5), complexes [3,2]/[3,1] for volume (OthClean+Jerk 8×[3,2]=38, m0w0d2), Press 80×2–5 + 60×1 filler. Tak: flat 3s (233×3, 199×2 vs ours 670×3/755×2 but Tak never >4 except Fund 6–7), complexes [1,1]/[2,1]/[2,2] and 1-3 ladder (m0w3d4 OthSnatch 60×1/60×3 alternating; SnPull+Snatch m1w0d4; OthSn+OthSq m1w0d2). Tak Fundamental 81 reps (70×7, 80×3–6) — ours zero.
6. [SEV-3] What we never do: any pull, any Fundamental, any pull+lift complex, any 1+1 C+J peaking (Tak m1w2d0–m1w2d4: 90×[1,1]×3), 90% squat doubles/triples at low volume. What Tak never does: 5-rep jerk complexes ([3,2]×6–8), 60×7 squat grinds, Press-as-filler (ours Press mean 74.5 but 60–70×1–2 + 80×2; Tak Press 70% @80×3–4 + Fund carriers), 100% singles (ours 3×100×1 incl C+J 100×[1,1] m4w2d5 — Tak max 90, correct for 8-wk pre-comp).
Verdict: block-vs-year explains weekly total and taper tail only. Squat-for-pulls swap, missing heaviness progression, and huge/medium polarization are ours-specific defects. Fix order: add pulls ~15%, cap squat share ~25%, shift classics modal 60→70 with real 90+ peak.
