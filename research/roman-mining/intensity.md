## 1. Intensity definitions (p39-40, p79-80)

- Load classes (p39 fn): minimal <60%; small 60–69.9%; medium 70–79.9%; large 80–89.9%; submaximum 90–97.5%; maximum 100%.
- Competition exercises = snatch and C&J with 95–100% (p40).
- Intensity = average weight (kg) = volume(kg)/lifts; per exercise, workout, week, month, year (p79).
- Intensity as %: snatch exercises + snatch pull ÷ limit snatch; all other exercises ÷ limit C&J (p79). Limit = made in training or competition (Tables 2–6 notes).
- Do NOT use best-in-each-exercise as 100% (e.g. power snatch % of power snatch best): breaks cross-athlete comparability (example 100kg snatcher with 80 vs 85 power snatch; 80% = 64 vs 68kg) and requires constant re-testing (p79).
- Zone grouping by 10% intervals: … 60–69.9% = 7th group; 70–79.9% = 8th; 80–89.9% = 9th; 90–99.9% = 10th; 100–109.9% = 11th; 110–119.9% = 12th; … 130–139.9% = 14th (p79-80).
- Multi-year: relative intensity ≈36–41% of biathlon total (avg 38.5%) light; 35–40% middle; ≈34–39% (avg 36.5%) heavy (p80).
- 3rd-stage (MSIC/WR) relative intensity stabilizes from Class I at avg 70% in competition exercises (p160).
- High-intensity work (1983–85 computer study, p160): 70%+ in snatch/snatch-ex./C&J/C&J-ex.; 100%+ (of limit C&J) in clean pulls and squats. Max volume by MS; decreases for MSIC/WR (70–79% drops; 80–89% and 90%+ stabilize) (p160).
- Correlation with result (p158): Group-1 exercises (snatch/C&J ex., front+back squats, clean pulls) avg-weight vs result r=0.381–0.478 (n=75–93); Group-2 (snatch pulls, presses, bend-overs, other squats) r=0.229–0.253; only moderate correlation r=0.518–0.528 is snatch/C&J/snatch+C&J ex. with 90%+.

### General strength method (p42-44)
- 1–3 reps/set (1–3 RM) = max strength gain, almost no working hypertrophy (neural coordination); 4–6 RM = hypertrophy + strength only slightly less; 7–10 RM = more mass, less strength (p42).
- Beginners/low-class: use submax reps (weight liftable 12× lifted 4–6×, i.e. 2–3 less than max); limit-RM sets fatigue/injure and cut volume (p43). Emphasize 3–6/set, up to 10 for individual muscle groups, with minimal/small/medium weights (p43).
- Qualified (I, CMS, MS) must combine multiple-rep method with brief-effort (1–3) method; systematic significant tension required or strength stalls/drops; 90–100% gives highest strength increase (p43-44).
- Higher-qualified (MSIC) need frequent 1–3 brief-effort; drop in usual weight halts strength; already "found weight class" so no more mass needed; to maintain mass, 100/110/110+kg do frequent multiple lifts (4+) at max/near-max reps per set (p44).
- Explosive single-effort must be trained daily at all stages (example: 100kg snatch 3–4× but failing 105 once = strength-endurance without single-effort ability); single-max-tension develops concentration + absolute strength (p44).
- Quickness developed with weights not distorting max-structure: 80–95% (p40). Most precise/stable lifts 85–95% → best for technique (p40). Large training share should be 80–95% (strength+speed+technique together), but constant departure needed: lighter/faster assistance (power snatch/clean, pulls, push-jerk — larger amplitude/speed) for speed; heavier assistance (pulls, squats) for strength; full range used 40–120% to avoid monotonous stereotype and CNS fatigue (p40-42). Prolonged only-very-large or only-small efforts fixate slow/weak patterns and block 95–100% display (p42).

### Snatch/C&J reps (p48, p57-60)
- One workout: 10–30 lifts in snatch + other snatch ex.; fewer with submax/max included (p48).
- Low-class: ≤5–6 lifts/workout with submax/max in snatch; 95% ≤3×; 100% 1–2 sets, only once per 7–10 days (p48).
- Qualified middleweight: ≈10 submax/max lifts in one workout; light more, heavy fewer. 95–100% basically over 2–3 days; 90% over one day, or 2–3 days in a row if few lifts (p48).
- C&J per workout ≈10–30 jerk-ex. lifts; fewer with submax/max (p57).
- Low-class C&J: ≤5 lifts with 90%, ≤3 with 95% in one workout; 100% once per 10–14 days (p57).
- Qualified middleweight: ≈10 submax/max lifts in one jerk ex. per workout; lighter more, heavier fewer. 95–100% in one jerk ex. basically after 2–3 days; 90% repeatable next day; different jerk ex. each day → 90% can go 2–3 days running (p57).
- C&J reps/set fewer than snatch (longer, heavier) (p58).
- Jerk from stands: if poor technique, +≤5% weight set-to-set; more reps with min/small, fewer with moderate/large (p59). Example ladder 60(50)/4×2 … 95(80)/1×2 (120 max) (p59).
- Jerk-at-end / power clean for strength-endurance: ≤85% max, 4–6 sets; e.g. 112.5(75)/4, 120(80)/3, 127.5(85)/2×2 (150); or 105(75)/4,2,6,1,5 (140) (p60).
- Combination ex. e.g. front squat+push-jerk 140(70)/2+2, 160(80)/2+2×3, 150(75)/2+2×2 (200); squat clean+front squat+jerk 90(45)/1+2+1 … 150(75)/1+2+1×2 (200) (p60-61).
- Push-press: alone or after press with same/somewhat heavier weight; ≤5–10 lifts at 50–80% limit C&J; fewer press lifts → more push-press and vice versa (p57). Variants: 60(50)/3, 70(60)/3, 85(70)/3×2, 90(75)/2×2 (120); 80(55)/4×2, 90(60)/3, 105(70)/2, 115(77.5)/1 (150); 120(65)/5×3 (185); 150(75)/2×3 (200) (p61).
- Power snatch before snatch: no min/small after power snatch, go straight to fundamental weights; 2–3 overhead squats in last two power-snatch sets (p50). 10–20 lifts if snatch or C&J second; small/moderate power-snatch weights if heavy snatch/C&J follows; else small→submax in power snatch, up to 25–30 lifts, in 2 series (start+end or middle+end; heavier mid/start than end) (p51-52). Beginners only start/middle first 2–3 months (p52).
- Pre-fatigue 6–15 power snatch/clean reps before C&J/clean: start C&J/clean 5–15kg above preceding ex.; prepare legs with squats in same style in last two power sets (p59).
- Jerk ex. in middle of workout = strength; at end = strength+endurance (p60). Mid: 90(75)/4, 95(80)/3, 102.5(85)/2×3 (120); end (on plinths): 77.5(65)/6×3–4 (120) (p60).

### Pulls reps (p63-64)
- Prep period: chiefly 2–6 reps/set with 80–89% and 90–99%, 2–5 with 100–109%. Comp period with 80–89/90–99/100–109%: chiefly 2–4. Lightweights often 2–3, heavyweights 4–6, middleweights 2–3 and 4–6. Outside those weights → 1–3/set. Comp period: 100%+ for 1 rep/set as often as possible (p63-64).
- Per workout: 15–30 pulls at 2–4/set; 35–50 at 5–6/set (p64). Variants e.g. 95%/3×8; 95%/4×7; 95%/5×6; 85%/6×5; 85%/5×5+90%/3×4; 75%/3, 85%/6, 95%/5, 105%/3×2, 115%/2×3 (p64).
- Pulls almost every workout; usually snatch pulls one workout, clean pulls next; both possible in one; usually middle/end of workout (p64). Vary: lower (15cm platform) / higher (15cm+ plinths) start, grip width, slow execution (p64).

### Squats reps (p67-68)
- Both prep+comp, front+back: 2–6 with 45–85%, 1–6 with 95–105%, 1–5 with 115%, 1–2 with 125% of limit C&J. Lightweights often 1–3, middleweights 1–4/set. 6–10/set primarily other squat types. More reps/set in prep than comp (p68).
- ≈20–30 squats/workout appropriate; up to ~60; split in two series if many (p68). Almost every workout (p68).
- Training-weight band: back+front ≈45–125%, other squats 35–55% of max C&J; lunges/split-leg heavyweight 15–25% limit C&J; fundamental squat weight 75–105% limit C&J (p67).
- Squat rhythm must match classic lifts (relatively smooth down at near-limit snatch/C&J weight, fast up; periodically rise on toes, lunges, competition foot placement) (p67-68).

### Presses / bend-overs / jumps / statics reps (p69-75)
- Standing/seated press weight 25–65% limit C&J (avg 37% prep, 40% comp); all classes ≈equal in prep, higher for heavier classes in comp (p69-70). Reps/set primarily 3–6; heavyweights to 8 (p70). ≈6–30 lifts/workout; 6–18 if jerks/push-jerks done, more if not (p70). Never heavy press day before submax/max C&J workout; presses middle/end of workout (p70). Variants listed p70-71 (standing 40/6×6 etc.; seated 35/5×5; bench 45/5+55/5×2; wide-grip behind-neck 25/6×5) (p70-71).
- No need for max press/bench results (indirect effect only); heavy standing press risks low back (p70).
- Good mornings ≈25–60% (avg 50%), hyperextensions 15–35% (avg 20%) limit C&J; good-morning volume ≈15–150/mo, hyperextension 20–600/mo; lightweights favor good mornings, heavyweights hyperextensions; bend-overs 3–10/set both periods; older/advanced use less; big comp-period bend-over load harms snatch/C&J (p71).
- Kettlebell behind neck 15–20% C&J ×6–10; horse sit-ups 5–20% ×4–10 (p72). Arm/shoulder accessories: overhead arm bends 10–25% C&J (discs 5–10kg beginners) ×3–10; dips BW→5–25% (to 40–50% if partial) ×3–10 (3–5 at 40–50%) (p72).
- Barbell jumps 20% C&J, ≈12–18/workout, 3–6/set, 2–3×/week; omitted if depth jumps used (p73). Depth jumps: first 3 weeks of comp period 3× each: wkt1–2: 2×10 from 0.5m; wkt3: 3×10/0.5m; wkt4: 4×10/0.5m; wkt5–9: 4×10/0.7m; same dosage usable in prep (p73).
- Isometric/slow/mixed: ≈10% of month volume both periods (≈½ in "stretches"); ≈50 lifts/week first 3 weeks both periods; almost every workout; heavyweights (esp. prep) can do more; lightweights careful (harm results — use mainly snatch+clean "stretches" for squat-under technique) (p74-76). Hold duration 5–6s (shorter/longer = less effect); slow lift 8–15s; slow lower 6–10s; lift+lower 15–30s; shorter if hold inside slow move. Weight ≈25% (snatch stretches) to 105% (pulls/squats) (p75). Holds at: start break, post-first-phase, pre-final-effort, full extension+toes, overhead fixation, 90° half-squat, bent-over horizontal (p75).
- Min jump with 50% BW for successful jerk (75–100kg classes): III-44, II-45, I-46, CMS-47, MS-48, MSIC-50, WR-51 cm; ≈2cm lower for lighter/heavier classes. E.g. Rigert 59, Kuznetsov (100) 62, Vardanyan (82.5) 65 cm (p73).

## 5. Intensity-wave rules (month → week → workout)

- Month distribution uneven: alternate large/small/moderate months; gradual rise only for beginners/low-class or post-layoff restart (p87-88). Comp-month cut vs preceding month 20–40% (avg 30%), larger for heavier lifters; e.g. 52kg −27.5%, 110+kg −33.5%; beginners −17–18% suffices (p93). Final week ≈11–14% of month volume in comp period (p105). MS prep→comp example: 2100→1500, 2000→1400+1200, 1600+2100→1500, 1600→1100 (p91-92).
- Week-variant notation (p105-106, Table 13): single figure (1/2/3) = peak week (rise to it, fall after); two figures (3-1 etc.) = first peak week, second slightly reduced. E.g. 1-3 = max week 1, down week 2, up week 3 (still < week 1), down week 4 (p105-106).

- Shock training (large volume+intensity) in comp month on same weekday as competition (p110).
- Submax/max placement (p108-110): distribute month's snatch+C&J submax/max over weeks 4 or 3 (if one week is recuperation). Basically first 3 comp weeks. If competed last week of prior month → reduce count, exclude/small in week 1. Not all (esp. heavyweights) can do 97.5–100% in training though >100% in comp → plan 90–95% for them (p109).

Lighter athletes later (closer to comp); longer recovery needed after C&J than snatch (p109). - Overtraining guard: include only-moderate/small lifts each week-cycle (p110).
- Final-week protocol (p110, p124-128, p140-141): low volume+low intensity; ≈11–14% month volume (p105); last-week-before-comp small (p124-125). Control max snatch+C&J in weeks 3–4 then recuperate to exceed in comp (p125). Contrast-directional volume×intensity preferred; each athlete selects best variant (p141). Heavyweights (incl. 90kg) exclude submax/max snatch+C&J in last week; do them weeks 1–3 (most favorable: snatch submax/max max in week 3, cleans week 2, jerks weeks 1–2, clean pulls week 1; comp-month clean-pull high-intensity 36/33/31% weeks 1–3) (p150-151).
- Superheavy comp-month timing (p154): all submax/max snatch+C&J in first 3 weeks; 100%+ clean pulls also first 3 weeks; good mornings only first 2 weeks; hyperextensions first 3 weeks (4th week max 1 day small 16–20) (p154).
- Two-a-day splits (p145-146): >100 lifts/workout → split; ≈200 lifts/day prep, 180 comp; morning +10 prep / +15 comp over evening (p145). Prep: snatch mainly morning; other snatch ~even; C&J 2× morning vs evening; other C&J more evening; most 70%+ snatch+C&J morning (p145). Comp: snatch + other snatch ≈2× morning; 70%+ uniform morning/evening; classic clean/jerk + other clean ~uniform; other jerk + 70%+ C&J mainly morning (p145-146). Squats (front+back incl. 100%+) ≈2× evening in prep, ~even (slightly more max evening) in comp; other squats morning for light/middle, evening for mid-heavy/heavy both periods; snatch pulls (incl. max) more morning both periods; clean pulls more evening; presses morning+evening both; good mornings/hyperextensions morning (p146).
