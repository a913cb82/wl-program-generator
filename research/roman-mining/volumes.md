Source: research/roman-transcription/chunk-1.md through chunk-5.md. Page refs = printed book pages (## pNN). No commentary; only source numbers.

## Units and zones (all levels)
- Volume counted in lifts (reps), not tonnage (p78). Tonnage = kg × lifts, given only as supplementary info (p78). Example: 100t month at 80kg avg = 1,250 lifts; at 100kg avg = 1,000 lifts (p78).
- Intensity = average weight = kg-volume / lifts; per-exercise, workout, week, month, year (p79). As %: snatch exercises + snatch pull ÷ limit snatch; all other exercises ÷ limit clean-and-jerk (p79). Alternative (rejected): % of best in each exercise (p79).
- Intensity zones grouped in 10% intervals: <10%, 10–19.9%, … 60–69.9% (7th), 70–79.9% (8th), 80–89.9% (9th), 90–99.9% (10th), 100–109.9% (11th), 110–119.9% (12th), 130–139.9% (14th) (p79–p80).
- Weight terms (p39): minimal <60%, small 60–69.9%, medium 70–79.9%, large 80–89.9%, submaximum 90–97.5%, maximum 100%. Competition exercises = snatch and C&J with 95–100% (p40 fn).
- Reps/set effect (p42–p43): 1–3 RM = max strength, ~no hypertrophy; 4–6 RM = hypertrophy + slightly less strength gain; 7–10 RM = more mass, less strength gain. RM defined p42. Beginners/low-class use 2–3 fewer than max possible per set (e.g. weight liftable 12× done 4–6×) (p43). Table 1 reps/set by % (p45–46): snatch/C&J/jerk: 50–59.9% 4–6, 60–69.9% 2–3, 70–79.9% 1–2, 80–89.9% 1; power snatch/power clean rows [?] in source; push-jerk 50–59.9% 4–5, 60–69.9% 3–4, 70–79.9% 1–2, 80–89.9% 1; front/back squat 50–59.9% 6–10, 60–69.9% 4–6, 70–79.9% 3–4, 80–89.9% 1–2, 90–100% 1; bench press 40–49.9% 6–10, 50–59.9% 5–8, 60–69.9% 4–6, 70–79.9% 3–4 (p46).
- Light-class reference conventions (p45): snatch Tables light=60kg class (52/56kg +slightly high-intensity lifts; 67.5kg −slightly, but not below middle classes); mid-heavy+1st heavy =100kg class (90kg +slightly; 110kg −slightly, bounded by 82.5kg and 110+); middle =75/82.5kg. Applies Tables 2–6.

## Year volumes (lifts/year) and month ranges
- Year totals (p80): beginners 10,000; low-class ~12,000; Class I 15,000; MS 18,000; MSIC less (than MS). Increase year-to-year by (1) more lifts/workout, (2) more workouts at same lifts/session, (3) both (p80).
- Research 1983–85, n=93 strongest (p159): year volume novice→MS 10,000→20,000 avg lifts; MSIC avg 18,000; world-record holders (WR) 16,200 (p159). Month volume in prep = avg 9% of year volume (p159).
- Month ranges (p88): beginners prep 1,100–1,300, comp 900–1,000; low-class prep 1,000–1,500, comp 750–1,050; Class I/CMS prep 1,300–2,000, comp 950–1,450; MS prep 1,500–2,700, comp 1,100–1,900. MSIC: ~200 fewer prep, ~150 fewer comp than MS; less variability (p88).
- Comp-month reduction vs prior month 20–40% avg 30%, greater for heavier lifters: 52kg −27.5%, 110+ −33.5%; beginners only −17–18% sufficient (p93).
- Intensity (% of biathlon total): light 36–41% avg 38.5%; middle 35–40%; heavy ~34–39% avg 36.5% (p80). Multi-year intensity example middleweight: end yr1 72kg, yr2 87kg (+15), yr3 98.5kg (+11.5), yr4 108kg (+9.5); yr7 +4.7, yr8 +3.8, then insignificant (p80).

## Intensity planning mechanics (month)
- High-intensity = 70%+ in snatch/C&J exercises; 100%+ in pulls/squats (p159–p160, Tables 2–6).
- MS middleweight example (p107): 2nd prep month high-intensity snatch+C&J = 443 lifts; comp month = 342. Hence moderate week = 111 prep / 86 comp; fewer = small, more = large (p107). Change week intensity by changing count of 70%+ lifts in snatch/C&J, clean pulls, squats (p107).
- Figure 19 (p52): improvement vs count of 70%+ lifts (comp period), MS solid / CMS dashed, by class group (p52). Figure 20 (p52): 70%+ count vs age 18–25 declining; −~20/month at 18, −10 at 20 vs baseline 21.5y; +10/+20 reduction at 23/25y (p47–p48). Applies Tables 2–6 athletes avg 21.5y (p47).
- Share of Figure-19 lifts by group (p47, p55): snatch share MS 44% light, 45% middle, 46% midheavy+1st heavy, 48% 2nd heavy; Class I/CMS 43/44/45/48%. Jerk share MS 56/55/54/52%; Class I/CMS 57/56/55/52% (p55).
- Figure 24 (p111): mean submax+max lifts/month in snatch and C&J exercises, MS/CMS/Class I/Class II-III, prep vs comp periods (graph only).
- Submax/max distribution (p108–p110): spread over wks 1–4 (or 1–3 if wk4 recuperation) prep; basically wks 1–3 comp. ≤35% of month's submax/max snatch+C&J in wk3; further increase allowed wk3 only for snatch; C&J increase no later than wk2 (p109). Not all (esp. heavyweights) can do 97.5–100% in training though 100%+ in comp; plan 90–95% for them (p109). Last-execution lag (p109–p110): snatch 90–92.5% 5–9d, 95–97% 6–12d, 100%+ 7–15d pre-comp; C&J 90–92.5% 5–13d, 95–97% 7–15d, 100%+ 9–19d. Lighter athletes later; C&J needs longer recovery than snatch (p109). Shock training in comp month same weekday as comp (p110). Squats 100–107.5% 6–10d, 110–117.5% 8–12d, 120%+ 10–16d; clean pulls 100–107.5% 8–12d, 110–117.5% 9–15d, 120%+ 11–19d; lighter athletes later (p110). Squats: ~94% of max lifts wks1–3, 6% wk4 comp (p110). Clean-pull max: ~36% wk1, 33% wk2, 31% wk3, none wk4 (p110).
